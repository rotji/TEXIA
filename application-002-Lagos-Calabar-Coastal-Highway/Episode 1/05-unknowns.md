# Unknowns

## 1. Purpose

This document records the important questions that remain unresolved in Episode 1 of the Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway investigation.

The purpose is to:

* prevent unresolved issues from being presented as facts;
* distinguish genuine unknowns from questions already answered;
* identify what evidence could resolve each unknown;
* prioritize future investigation;
* prevent the investigation from expanding endlessly without purpose;
* create a controlled handoff into later episodes.

The governing principle is:

```text id="0q3p6m"
UNKNOWN
   ↓
WHY IT MATTERS
   ↓
WHAT EVIDENCE COULD RESOLVE IT?
   ↓
WHERE CAN THAT EVIDENCE BE FOUND?
   ↓
PRIORITY
```

---

# 2. Unknown Status

### OPEN

The question remains unresolved and requires investigation.

### PARTIALLY RESOLVED

Some elements are established, but important uncertainty remains.

### BLOCKED

The investigation has identified the required evidence, but that evidence is currently inaccessible or unavailable.

### RESOLVED

Sufficient evidence has answered the question.

### DEFERRED

The question is legitimate but falls outside the scope of the current episode.

## UNKNOWN 001 — Why 700 km vs 750 km?

### Question

Why is the project described as approximately **700 km** in some documents and approximately **750 km** in later descriptions?

### Why it matters

Project length is one of the most basic descriptions of the highway.

A change of approximately 50 km could represent:

* route modification;
* additional scope;
* revised section structure;
* measurement differences;
* another project-definition change.

### Evidence needed

* earliest official project description;
* later official project descriptions;
* dated route maps;
* section-by-section lengths;
* revised project documents;
* engineering descriptions.

### Current status

**OPEN**

### Priority

**HIGH**

# 13. Unknowns That Should NOT Become Endless Research

Not every unanswered question deserves immediate investigation.

A question should enter the active investigation queue only if resolving it could materially change:

* the documentary's factual account;
* an important claim;
* the understanding of what changed;
* the interpretation of the project;
* the public's ability to independently inspect the evidence;
* the methodology itself.

Otherwise it should remain deferred.

---

# 14. Unknown Priority System

### P1 — Critical

Must be resolved or explicitly disclosed before making the associated claim.

Examples:

* project scope;
* major financing claims;
* completion claims;
* major route claims.

### P2 — Important

Could materially improve the investigation but does not prevent Episode 1 publication.

Examples:

* exact construction quantities;
* detailed section configuration;
* additional independent imagery.

### P3 — Later Investigation

Important, but belongs primarily to a future episode.

Examples:

* detailed property mapping;
* compensation;
* historical alignment reconstruction;
* cable geometry.

### P4 — Deferred

Interesting but currently not necessary to answer the investigation's core question.

# 15. Episode 1 Unknown Boundary

Episode 1 does not need to resolve every unknown.

It needs to clearly establish:

```text id="4n6k3n"
WHAT WE KNOW
     +
WHAT WE DON'T KNOW
     +
WHY WE DON'T KNOW IT
     +
WHAT EVIDENCE WOULD RESOLVE IT
```

This allows the investigation to continue without pretending that the first episode has answered everything.

---

# 16. Unknown → Future Investigation Rule

A future episode should not be created simply because an unknown exists.

A future episode should be triggered when:

```text id="3c5x89"
UNKNOWN
   ↓
MATERIAL QUESTION
   ↓
AVAILABLE / OBTAINABLE EVIDENCE
   ↓
REAL INVESTIGATIVE VALUE
   ↓
PUBLIC INTEREST
   ↓
NEW EPISODE JUSTIFIED
```

For example:

```text id="6kn7p4"
ALIGNMENT UNKNOWN
      ↓
OBTAIN GAZETTE
      +
ENGINEERING MAP
      +
CURRENT CENTRELINE
      ↓
RECONSTRUCT ROUTE
      ↓
MEANINGFUL FINDINGS
      ↓
EPISODE 2
```

# 18. Final Principle

An unknown should never silently become a fact.

The investigation must preserve the boundary:

```text id="2p7r3s"
EVIDENCE
   ↓
KNOWN
   │
   ├── PARTIALLY KNOWN
   │
   ├── REPORTED
   │
   └── UNKNOWN
```

The goal is not to eliminate every unknown before publication.

The goal is to make the boundary between **known and unknown visible**.
