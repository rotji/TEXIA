# Evidence Plan

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the evidence required to establish the major factual propositions in Episode 1 of the Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway investigation.

The purpose is not to collect as many documents or sources as possible.

The purpose is to establish a traceable relationship between:

```text
QUESTION
   ↓
PROPOSITION
   ↓
REQUIRED EVIDENCE
   ↓
SOURCE
   ↓
EVIDENCE LOCATION
   ↓
VERIFICATION
   ↓
LIMITATION
```

Evidence should be sufficient to support the proposition being made, while clearly distinguishing established facts, reported claims, observations, interpretations, and unresolved questions.

---

# 2. Evidence Status

Each proposition will receive one of the following statuses:

### Established

There is sufficient reliable evidence to state the proposition as a fact.

### Corroborated

The proposition is supported by multiple genuinely independent sources or evidence types.

### Reported

A credible source reports the proposition, but independent verification is incomplete.

### Observed

The condition or event has been directly observed through physical evidence, imagery, video, documents, or other primary evidence.

### Partially Established

Some parts of the proposition are established, but important details remain unresolved.

### Unresolved

Available evidence is insufficient to determine the answer.

### Contradictory

Reliable sources or evidence materially disagree and the disagreement has not been resolved.

---

# 3. Evidence Rule

A source does not automatically establish everything it claims.

For every important proposition we record:

```text
WHAT IS BEING CLAIMED?
        ↓
WHAT DOES THE SOURCE ACTUALLY SHOW?
        ↓
WHAT CAN WE SAFELY CONCLUDE?
        ↓
WHAT CAN WE NOT CONCLUDE?
```

A government statement, for example, may establish that a government official made a statement.

It does not automatically establish that the underlying statement is factually correct.

Likewise, a construction photograph may establish that construction activity was occurring at a particular location and date.

It does not automatically establish the percentage completion of the entire project.

# 4. Episode 1 Evidence Matrix

## 4.1 Project Identity

### Proposition

The Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway is a federal infrastructure project intended to connect Lagos with Calabar along the coastal corridor.

### Required evidence

* official project description;
* Federal Government/Federal Ministry of Works documentation;
* project approval or announcement;
* official project maps or section descriptions.

### What evidence should establish

* project existence;
* responsible government institution;
* stated purpose;
* general corridor;
* project scope as described at the relevant date.

### Limitation

The stated purpose does not by itself establish the project's eventual configuration or final completion.

### Status

Established.

---

# 4.2 Project Approval

### Proposition

The Federal Executive Council approved construction of the Lagos–Port Harcourt–Calabar Coastal Superhighway project in February 2024, with the initial Lagos section awarded to Hitech.

### Required evidence

* FEC announcement/report;
* Federal Ministry of Works documentation;
* contract information;
* official project announcement.

### What evidence should establish

* approval date;
* approved project;
* contractor;
* relevant section;
* approved contract value where available.

### Limitation

Approval establishes authorization of the project/contract. It does not establish that construction was completed or that all approved funding was immediately available.

### Status

Established.

---

# 4.14 Construction Progress Claims

### Question

What construction progress can actually be established at each stage?

### Required evidence

* dated government progress statements;
* dated independent photographs/videos;
* satellite imagery where useful;
* construction documents;
* commissioning/opening records.

### Required rule

Every percentage must identify:

```text
PERCENTAGE
    +
WHAT IT MEASURES
    +
SECTION/SCOPE
    +
DATE
    +
SOURCE
```

### Example

Do not write:

> "The Lagos–Calabar Highway is 70% complete."

Unless the evidence establishes that this percentage applies to the entire project.

Instead determine whether it means:

> Section 1 is 70% complete as of a particular date.

### Status

Partially established.

# 5. Project Identity and Geometry Evidence

Episode 1 does not attempt to fully resolve the historical alignment dispute, but the project configuration must be represented accurately.

## Required evidence

* official project maps;
* Section 1 ESIA;
* engineering drawings;
* section maps;
* dated alignment descriptions.

### Known evidence

The Section 1 ESIA documents multiple alignment alternatives and a redesigned/optimized alignment.

### What this establishes

* alignment alternatives existed;
* alignment was subject to design evaluation;
* environmental, infrastructure, traffic, safety and community considerations were part of the design process.

### What it does not establish

It does not by itself establish that:

```text
2006 Gazetted Alignment
=
First Proposed Alignment
```

or:

```text
2006 Gazetted Alignment
=
Optimized Alignment
```

or:

```text
Government "Old Alignment"
=
ESIA First Proposed Alignment
```

Those relationships require geometry.

### Status

Partially established.

# 7. Physical Evidence

Physical evidence should be used to answer:

> What can actually be seen happening on the ground?

Potential evidence:

* dated construction photographs;
* video footage;
* satellite imagery;
* road-opening evidence;
* bridge construction;
* pavement;
* earthworks;
* drainage;
* barriers;
* traffic use;
* construction equipment;
* project signage.

### Physical evidence rule

Every physical observation should record:

```text
DATE
LOCATION
SOURCE
WHAT IS VISIBLE
WHAT IT ESTABLISHES
WHAT IT DOES NOT ESTABLISH
```

Example:

```text
Observation:
Pavement construction visible at a specific location.

Establishes:
Construction activity was occurring there at the observation date.

Does not establish:
Overall section completion percentage.
```

# 9. Evidence Quality Test

Before an important proposition enters the final script, apply:

```text
SOURCE IDENTIFIED?
        ↓ YES
DATE IDENTIFIED?
        ↓ YES
ORIGINAL EVIDENCE LOCATED?
        ↓ YES
SOURCE LINEAGE CHECKED?
        ↓ YES
PROPOSITION MATCHES EVIDENCE?
        ↓ YES
LIMITATIONS IDENTIFIED?
        ↓ YES
COMPETING EVIDENCE CHECKED?
        ↓ YES
VERIFICATION STATUS ASSIGNED?
        ↓ YES
READY FOR SCRIPT
```

If any critical step fails, the proposition should be downgraded or marked unresolved.

# 11. Final Evidence Principle

The investigation should never ask:

> "Do we have enough sources?"

It should ask:

> "Do we have enough evidence to support exactly what we are saying?"

The standard is:

```text
CLAIM
 ↓
EVIDENCE
 ↓
WHAT IT PROVES
 ↓
WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE
 ↓
VERIFICATION STATUS
```

No uncertainty should be removed merely to make the documentary narrative cleaner.
