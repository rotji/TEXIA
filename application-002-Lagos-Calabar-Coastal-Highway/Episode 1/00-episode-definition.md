# Episode Definition

## Working title

**Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway: What Is Actually Being Built?**

## Episode purpose

Episode 1 establishes what the Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway project is, how it began, how its configuration and financing context have developed, what construction has been verified, and what remains unresolved.

The episode must present only information supported by established evidence or evidence subsequently verified during the investigation.

## Central question

> **What is actually happening with the Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway, and how has the project changed over time?**

## Scope

### Included

* Project identity and stated purpose
* Verified history
* Major verified changes
* Project sections and configuration
* Contract and contractor context
* EPC + Finance structure
* Financing developments
* Verified construction progress
* Current verified state
* Questions that justify continuing the investigation

### Reserved for later episodes

* Detailed alignment analysis
* Okun-Ajah/Winhomes
* Detailed demolition investigation
* Detailed compensation investigation
* Detailed money-trail investigation
* Complete procurement investigation
* Exact historical route reconstruction

## Evidence boundary

Every public statement in Episode 1 must be supported by established evidence or subsequently verified evidence.

Where sources disagree, the disagreement must be presented rather than silently resolved.

Claims, observations, official statements, reported allegations, inferences, and verified facts must not be presented as equivalent forms of evidence.

## Uncertainty boundary

Unresolved issues must remain explicitly unresolved.

Episode 1 must not present conclusions about matters that the available evidence does not establish, including:

* The exact final total length of the project
* The complete evolution of the project's section structure
* The exact relationship between different historical and current alignments
* Exact expenditure or disbursement beyond what the available evidence establishes
* Overall project completion based on incompatible or undefined percentages
* Issues reserved for later investigations

## Episode completion criteria

* [ ] The project is introduced clearly.
* [ ] The history is supported by verified evidence.
* [ ] Major changes are identified without overstating them.
* [ ] Financing, contract value, disbursement, expenditure, and construction delivery are not conflated.
* [ ] Construction progress is presented only where the underlying measurement is understood.
* [ ] The current state is separated into established facts, claims, and unknowns.
* [ ] Important contradictions or unresolved differences are identified.
* [ ] Continuing questions are clearly stated.
* [ ] The episode does not prematurely resolve investigations reserved for later episodes.
