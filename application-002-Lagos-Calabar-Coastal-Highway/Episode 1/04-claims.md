# Claims

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the factual claims that Episode 1 is permitted to make.

Each claim must be connected to evidence and assigned a verification status.

The purpose is to prevent the documentary from:

* turning allegations into facts;
* turning announcements into completed events;
* confusing financing with expenditure;
* confusing one section with the entire highway;
* treating repeated reporting as independent corroboration;
* presenting unresolved questions as settled conclusions;
* removing uncertainty simply to make the story easier to tell.

The governing rule is:

```text
CLAIM
  ↓
SOURCE
  ↓
EVIDENCE
  ↓
WHAT IT ESTABLISHES
  ↓
LIMITATION
  ↓
STATUS
```

---

# 2. Claim Statuses

### ESTABLISHED

Evidence is sufficient to state the claim as a factual proposition.

### CORROBORATED

The proposition is established and supported by genuinely independent evidence.

### REPORTED

A credible source reports the proposition, but independent verification is incomplete.

### OBSERVED

The proposition is directly supported by physical, visual, documentary, or other primary observation.

### PARTIALLY ESTABLISHED

The central proposition is supported, but important details remain unresolved.

### UNRESOLVED

Available evidence is insufficient to determine the answer.

### CONTRADICTORY

Relevant evidence materially conflicts and the conflict has not yet been resolved.

### Overall Evidence Assessment

The February 2024 Federal Executive Council approval is **ESTABLISHED** based primarily on the 26 February 2024 Government of Nigeria publication reporting the FEC decision.

The Federal Ministry of Works publication from March 2024 provides additional government documentation showing that construction subsequently commenced and that the first phase began with approximately 47.47 km in Lagos.

These sources are from the same broad government information system and therefore should not be treated as fully independent corroboration.

### Limitation

Federal approval establishes that the project was approved at that point in time. It does **not** establish subsequent construction progress, completion, expenditure, financing, final route configuration, or the current state of the project.

# 8. Claim Update Rule

When new evidence appears:

```text
NEW EVIDENCE
      ↓
COMPARE WITH EXISTING CLAIM
      ↓
SAME STATE?
      │
      ├── YES → CORROBORATION
      │
      └── NO
            ↓
       WHAT CHANGED?
            ↓
       CORRECTION?
       NEW STATE?
       CLARIFICATION?
       CONTRADICTION?
       DIFFERENT SCOPE?
       DIFFERENT MEASUREMENT?
            ↓
       UPDATE CLAIM STATUS
```

A later source does not automatically make an earlier source false.

# 10. Final Claim Standard

The documentary does not need to make every possible claim.

It needs to make the **right claims**.

The standard is:

```text
CAN WE SHOW IT?
     ↓
CAN WE SOURCE IT?
     ↓
CAN WE DATE IT?
     ↓
CAN WE EXPLAIN WHAT IT ACTUALLY ESTABLISHES?
     ↓
CAN WE STATE ITS LIMITATION?
     ↓
CAN WE DEFEND IT IF CHALLENGED?
```

If the answer is no, the claim should be:

```text
QUALIFIED
REPORTED
PARTIAL
OR UNRESOLVED
```

rather than presented as fact.

---

# 11. Documentary Principle

The objective of Episode 1 is not to prove a predetermined conclusion.

It is to reconstruct the most defensible account of the project's development that the available evidence allows as of **10 September 2026**.

Where evidence ends, the documentary should say that the evidence ends.

That boundary is itself part of the investigation.
