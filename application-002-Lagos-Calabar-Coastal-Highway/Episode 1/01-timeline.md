# Episode 1 Timeline

## Purpose

This timeline records the major events and state changes relevant to Episode 1 of the Lagos–Calabar Coastal Highway investigation.

It separates:

- event date from publication date
- what happened from what was announced or reported
- project state from knowledge of that state
- financing from disbursement and expenditure
- construction progress from completion claims

The timeline does not attempt to resolve disputed issues that belong to later episodes.

**Evidence boundary:** 10 September 2026

## Project Configuration Timeline

The project configuration cannot currently be represented as a simple linear sequence because different official and public sources describe different lengths and section structures at different times.

### Earlier configuration

Earlier official project descriptions referred to a highway of approximately **700 km**.

Section descriptions included:

- Section 1 — Lagos: approximately 47.474 km
- Section 2 — Lagos/Ogun: approximately 55.6 km
- Section 3A — Cross River: approximately 38 km
- Section 3B — Akwa Ibom: approximately 27 km
- Section 4 — described separately in earlier project documentation

### Later/current descriptions

Later government sources referred to the wider highway as approximately **750 km**.

Later section descriptions introduced or expanded configurations including:

- Section 1
- Section 2
- Sections 3A and 3B
- Sections 4A and 4B
- Section 5 / Akwa Ibom configurations

In May 2026, government also announced an approximately **82.8 km Akwa Ibom alignment**.

By August 2026, Ministry of Works statements described additional Akwa Ibom section lengths/configurations that do not yet map cleanly onto the earlier descriptions.

### Current status of the configuration question

The following are established:

- The coastal highway project exists.
- The general Lagos–Calabar corridor exists.
- The project has been divided into multiple sections.
- The section structure has changed or been described differently over time.
- Government sources have used both approximately 700 km and approximately 750 km descriptions.
- An approximately 82.8 km Akwa Ibom alignment was announced in May 2026.

The following remain unresolved:

- Why the overall project length changed from approximately 700 km to approximately 750 km.
- The exact evolution of the section structure.
- Whether every later section designation represents a new physical alignment, a subdivision/reclassification, an extension, or a combination of these.
- The exact relationship between the approximately 82.8 km Akwa Ibom alignment and later Akwa Ibom section descriptions.
- The final project configuration as of 10 September 2026.
